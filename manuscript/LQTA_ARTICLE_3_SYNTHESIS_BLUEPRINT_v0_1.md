# LQTA ARTICLE 3 — SYNTHESIS BLUEPRINT
## Working title
### Nonhomogeneous Relational Time and Endogenous Structural Physics

## Central thesis

The homogeneous temporal sector admits a common metrological calibration `r0`.

Nonhomogeneity is not fundamentally introduced by assigning an autonomous physical rate to each subsystem. Instead, it is encoded by local relational scale comparisons CAL.

When those comparisons are integrable, local rates `r_i` are reconstructed and the older clock language is recovered.

When they are not integrable, a cycle-supported residual `eta` remains that cannot be assigned to any collection of individual clocks. This residual defines irreducibly relational temporal memory.

The realized reorganization of that memory admits an exact provenance-selected balance law, while changes of relational support admit a restricted defect algebra. No autonomous CAL generator is derived.

## Architecture

1. Closed homogeneous baseline and common `r0`.
2. CAL as the nonhomogeneous comparison structure.
3. Exact/integrable sector and derived `r_i`.
4. Cycle obstruction, `eta`, and `Rho`.
5. Tree rigidity and emergence of irreducible temporal memory only with redundancy.
6. Realized changes and provenance continuity.

9. `kappa` defect algebra from an integrable ancestor.
10. Restricted relative source and the cross-carrier no-go.
11. Generator underdetermination.
12. Synthetic interpretation and scope.

## One-sentence interpretation

**Local clocks describe relational time only when scale comparison is globally integrable; once redundant relations carry nontrivial holonomy, temporal structure becomes collective and cannot be reduced to properties of individual clocks.**

## Claims to avoid

- CAL support physical space;
- `R_eff` electrical resistance;
- source sign a thermodynamic arrow;
- holonomy a Lorentz curvature;
- `kappa` a force;
- the structural theory an autonomous dynamics.
